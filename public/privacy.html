Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

This is a starting draft, not legal advice. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. If you have users in the EU/UK, GDPR imposes specific obligations (lawful basis documentation, data processing agreements with each processor below, breach notification timelines) that go beyond this draft — have it reviewed before relying on it.

1. What we collect

  • Account data: email address, and name if you sign in with Google.
  • Race telemetry & radio log: session summaries (sim, track, finishing position, lap count, fuel usage) and the text of what your engineer said during sessions, saved so you can review them in your dashboard.
  • Usage data: session counts, current plan, and gamification progress (streaks, badges).
  • Payment data: handled entirely by Stripe. We receive your subscription status and plan, not your card number.
  • Team data: if you're on the Team plan, the email addresses you add to your roster.

2. What we don't collect

The daemon reads telemetry locally on your PC to generate live commentary. Raw, frame-by-frame telemetry is not sent to our servers — only the session summary described above, once your session ends.

3. Who we share data with

We use the following processors to run the Service. Each has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data on our behalf.

ProcessorPurpose
SupabaseDatabase, authentication, and storage for your account, sessions, and radio log.
StripePayment processing for Pro and Team subscriptions.
GroqProcesses session telemetry and your spoken questions to generate the AI engineer's responses (speech-to-text, language model, text-to-speech context).
GoogleOptional sign-in (OAuth) if you choose "Continue with Google".

We do not sell your data to third parties or use it for advertising.

4. Where your data is processed

Our processors above operate infrastructure that may be located outside your country of residence, including in the United States. Where required, transfers rely on the safeguards those providers publish (e.g. standard contractual clauses).

5. How long we keep it

Account and session data is kept for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, we delete associated personal data within a reasonable period, except where we're required to retain records (e.g. billing history) by law.
